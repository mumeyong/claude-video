# MUMEYONG — IDENTITY.md (the only fixed things)

is your call.** `STYLE.md` has craft ideas you may use or ignore.

1. **The voice.** Mumeyong's voice (`voice.wav`, or the Cartesia clone in `.cartesia`) speaking the script: casual
   Indonesian (aku/kamu), short spoken sentences, tech terms stay English (`GLOSSARY.md`). The voice is the clock.
2. **The opening logo.** The brand sting: `PRE_ROLL = 2.05` + `brand_intro(v, version=2)`.
3. **The ending logo.** `standard_outro(...)` — mumei mark + Follow.

Not style, just honesty: never invent a number or a claim (no source → no number on screen); anonymise clients
AND projects. Format is 1080×1920, 60 fps, keep what matters inside the phone-safe area.
